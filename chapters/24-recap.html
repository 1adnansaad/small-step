“Achieving safe, climate resilient and prosperous delta”

BDP 2100 — Vision

The six goals

  1. Safety from floods and climate change related disasters.
  2. Water security and efficiency of water usages.
  3. Sustainable, integrated river systems and estuaries management.
  4. Conservation of wetlands and ecosystems, and their wise use.
  5. Effective institutions and equitable governance, in-country and transboundary.
  6. Optimal and integrated use of land and water resources.

The six hotspots

  • Coastal Zone — 27,738 sq. km
  • Barind & Drought Prone Areas — 22,848 sq. km
  • Haor & Flash Flood Areas — 16,574 sq. km
  • Chattogram Hill Tracts — 13,295 sq. km
  • River System & Estuaries — 35,204 sq. km
  • Urban Areas — 19,823 sq. km

The numbers that matter

70% of the country lies under one metre above sea level
−1.1% of GDP per year lost to climate change under moderate conditions; −2.0% under extreme
US$ 741bn cumulative income lost by FY2041 without the plan
2.5% of GDP per year needed for delta spending — up from 0.8% today
80 projects in the first Investment Plan, to 2030
US$ 37bn total capital cost of that plan (BDT 2,978 billion)

The idea in one sentence

Set a vision for 2100, group the country by the risks it actually faces, choose 'no regret' strategies that work across several possible futures, fund them at 2.5% of GDP, put one institution in charge of coordinating them — and revisit the whole thing every five years as the delta and the climate reveal what they are actually doing.
