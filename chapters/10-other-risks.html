Step 10 · Three more pressures

Upstream, pollution and waterlogging

Not every delta challenge falls from the sky. Three of the biggest come from decisions made upstream, in factories, and in how cities are built.

1 · What happens upstream

Bangladesh is highly dependent on developments upstream: the diversion, use or storage of flows from transboundary rivers is of major importance. It shows up in dry and monsoon season flows, salinisation, siltation of rivers, and sediment deposition in the Meghna estuary — which in turn decides whether the coastal floodplains can keep pace with sea level rise.

Most negatively impacted major rivers

The Ganges is the most negatively impacted, followed by the Brahmaputra, and to a much lesser extent the Meghna.

Most impacted regional rivers

The Dudhkumar, Dharla, Teesta and Mahananda.

2 · Water quality

32 rivers where water quality worsens severely — at risk of severe environmental degradation
12.6% of supply water contains arsenic

Industrialisation, mechanisation of agriculture, urbanisation and salinisation are all expected to deteriorate surface water quality further. Key groundwater quality issues are arsenic and salinisation, and pollution from industrial and domestic sources is a clear risk.

Three direct impacts of water pollution

  1. Increasing health problems for the rural and urban population.
  2. Loss of agricultural and industrial productivity from pollution and salt water intrusion.
  3. Environmental degradation, which degrades the environmental services the ecosystem provides.

3 · Waterlogging

Waterlogging in urban and rural areas is a serious development challenge, caused by unplanned and ineffective drainage infrastructure, encroachment on wetlands, and the hampering of tidal flows in coastal areas.

Where it bites hardest The south-west — Satkhira, Jashore, Khulna and Bagerhat — and the south-east — Noakhali and Feni.

Waterlogging is worth noticing because, unlike sea level rise, it is largely self-inflicted: it is the product of how drainage and wetlands have been managed. That also makes it one of the most fixable items in the plan.
