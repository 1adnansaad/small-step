Step 12 · Salient features

The vision and the six goals

Halfway. You have seen what the delta offers and what threatens it. This is the answer the plan gives.

“Achieving safe, climate resilient and prosperous delta”

BDP 2100 — Vision

“Ensure long term water and food security, economic growth and environmental sustainability while effectively reducing vulnerability to natural disasters and building resilience to climate change and other delta challenges through robust, adaptive and integrated strategies, and equitable water governance”.

BDP 2100 — Mission

To be implementable, that vision is translated into specific goals. It combines the long term development outcomes of the Perspective of 2041 — growth and poverty reduction — with targets for reducing vulnerability to water and climate hazards, plus targets for environmental conservation.

Three higher level national goals

  1. Eliminate extreme poverty by 2030.
  2. Achieve upper middle income status by 2030.
  3. Being a prosperous country beyond 2041.

Six BDP 2100 specific goals

These are the goals the Delta Plan owns. Every strategy and every project in the investment plan must contribute to at least one of them.

  1. Ensure safety from floods and climate change related disasters.
  2. Enhance water security and efficiency of water usages.
  3. Ensure sustainable and integrated river systems and estuaries management.
  4. Conserve and preserve wetlands and ecosystems and promote their wise use.
  5. Develop effective institutions and equitable governance for in-country and trans-boundary water resources management.
  6. Achieve optimal and integrated use of land and water resources.
How the two sets connect The six specific goals are not parallel to the national goals — they are the route to them. Bangladesh does not reach upper middle income status while losing 1.1% of GDP a year to a delta it cannot manage.
