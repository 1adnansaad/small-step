Step 3 · Analytical framework

From river basins to risk zones

Before the plan can decide what to build, it has to decide how to divide the country. BDP 2100 does it by risk, not by administration.

The Bangladesh Delta is defined in many ways. BDP 2100 uses the most expansive definition — one that takes in almost every district in the country, because nearly all of them face weather and climate risk from their position: near the sea, along a major river, or inside a water-scarce zone.

Only six districts sit outside Just six districts are relatively less hazard prone, thanks to being far from both the sea and the active rivers.

How the zones were drawn

  1. Start Take the country's eight hydrological zones as the starting point — hydrology drives everything in a delta, so it drives the planning regions too.
  2. Sharpen Focus on the magnitude of natural hazard vulnerability facing each hydrological region.
  3. Regroup Reorganise districts and areas that face similar risks into groups. These groups are the hotspots.
  4. Go deeper Because risk varies sharply even between neighbouring districts, a risk profile was also developed at district level and, where needed, at upazila level.

What a hotspot is — and isn't

A hotspot is simply a broad grouping of districts and areas facing similar natural hazard and climate change risks. It is a convenient way to summarise shared socio-economic characteristics and common risk profiles.

A first step, not the answer Hotspots are only the first step of analysis. The risk profile and the scale of vulnerability vary considerably between districts inside a hotspot, and between hotspots — which is why the district and upazila profiles matter.

Because the groupings overlap geographically, some districts fall into more than one hotspot — a coastal district crossed by a major river belongs to both.

BDP 2100 is, at heart, an adaptive techno-economic plan built on how water, land use, ecosystems and climate change interact with development outcomes. The six hotspots are how that interaction gets a map.
